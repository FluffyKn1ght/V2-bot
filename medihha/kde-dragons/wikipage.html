< Promo | Material

Konqi is KDE's mascot. When fun is needed, Konqi and other colorful KDE dragons are ready to help! Here you'll find artwork featuring Konqi and other KDE dragons. You can also upload your own or request new ones!


Want to learn more about Konqi and the KDE dragons? Visit Konqi's page!


Usage on KDE.News

When preparing articles for KDE.News ("the Dot"), it's recommended to add an abstract artwork to enhance visual appeal.

Abstract artwork appears in the top-right corner of the first paragraph -- typically a screenshot, photo, or icon. If no suitable image is available, Konqi and other KDE dragons are happy to help! See how it works on Konqi's Wikipedia page.

For KDE.News articles, click Edit summary to adjust the abstract text. Make sure both the abstract text and artwork appear on the front page.


Uploading your own artwork

You can upload your own KDE Dragon artwork to this page.

We welcome your own interpretation of the KDE Dragons. There is no limit on art style, and you don't need to follow Tyson Tan's design strictly. However, matching his style helps maintain branding consistency.

Before uploading, please read the guidelines and how-tos below.


Guidelines

  • Artwork must be your own work.
  • Artwork must be licensed under Creative Commons BY-SA, GFDL and LGPL.
  • If artwork is based on existing work, the source must be either:
    1. Public domain or licensed under Creative Commons Zero.
    2. Licensed under Creative Commons BY-SA, GFDL or LGPL.
    3. Compatible with any of the above.

Note: Low-quality uploads may be removed!


How to upload

  1. Register a KDE Identity
  2. Login with Phabricator on KDE Community Wiki
  3. Upload your file
  4. Edit this page and add your artwork to "Artworks by other artist" sections below.

Note: Can't figure it out? Ask a KDE Community member for help!
